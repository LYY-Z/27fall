## 45–60 秒：Why MUST AI?

> I am applying to the MSc in Artificial Intelligence because I want to move from practical AI deployment to a stronger understanding of machine learning, mathematics and evaluation. I built an edge-vision and irrigation system using YOLOv5, RK3588, a LangGraph decision chain and a local device state machine. This gave me a complete application loop, but it also raised questions about model reliability and safe action selection that I want to study more systematically. The programme’s foundations in machine learning, mathematics and deep learning, together with its thesis component, match that next step. I hope to apply the training to AI components for robotics and intelligent systems.

## 对论文制项目的准备

如被问“你为什么愿意做 thesis”：

> My practical projects have shown me problems worth studying, but I have not yet completed a substantial independent research study. A thesis would help me learn how to review literature, define a research question, design a reproducible evaluation and interpret the results. I would begin with a scope that matches the available data, supervision and time.

可讨论的初步方向：视觉模型在边缘设备上的延迟/精度取舍；不确定观测如何影响机器人任务决策；AI 建议与规则安全检查如何协同。它们都是**意向方向**，不是已确定论文题目。

## 可能追问与英文答法

### What do you want to learn from Foundations of Machine Learning?

> I want to understand the main learning settings, model assumptions and evaluation methods. In my edge-vision project, I used an inference model, but I want to reason more carefully about data, generalization, confidence and how to choose a model for a task.

### What AI topic needs the most mathematical preparation?

> I want to strengthen linear algebra, probability and optimization. These topics appear in model representation, uncertainty and training. I would review the foundations early and use small implementation exercises to connect the equations with observed model behaviour.

### What does responsible AI mean in a physical system?

> It means more than choosing an accurate model. The system should account for uncertainty, privacy and potential harm, explain which component is authorized to act, and provide a safe response when inputs are invalid or the model is uncertain. For an actuator, the AI output should pass through explicit policy and hardware checks.

### How would you plan a thesis?

> I would first read the literature and define one question that can be answered within the programme period. I would establish a baseline, specify the data and metrics, plan repeatable experiments and document negative results as well as positive ones. I would refine the plan with my supervisor before claiming a contribution.

### Why apply to both AI and robotics-related programmes?

> My long-term application area is robotics, but the MSc in AI would strengthen the learning and evaluation methods I want to use in robot systems. My robotics experience gives me a real application context, while this programme would deepen a different part of my preparation.
