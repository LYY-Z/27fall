# 01｜香港理工大学：MSc in Intelligent Robotics Engineering

## 面试前先确认

材料包里使用的项目名是 **MSc in Intelligent Robotics Engineering**。PolyU 当前官方项目页显示 **Sept 2027 Entry**，申请日期分为 early round（2026-10-20）与 main round（2027-02-25）。官方页列出 Embodied Robot Intelligence、Principles of Robotic Mechanisms、Robot Motion Planning、Advanced Control Technology、Soft Robotics 和 Advanced Product Mechatronics。面试时使用系统/邀请信中的实际批次和专业名。[2027 官方项目页](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/study/pg/tpg/2027/43101-ir-irt)

## 45–60 秒：Why PolyU and this programme?

> I am applying to the MSc in Intelligent Robotics Engineering because it connects the areas I have worked on separately: robot mechanisms, control, motion planning and embodied intelligence. My experience includes balance control on a ballbot, closed-chain leg control, mobile-robot navigation and dual-arm teleoperation. These projects gave me practical exposure, but I want a more systematic understanding of the mechanics and theory behind them. I am particularly interested in Advanced Control Technology, Robot Motion Planning and Principles of Robotic Mechanisms, because they match clear gaps in my experience. I hope to use the programme to connect mathematical models with experiments and develop into a robotics algorithm engineer in the Greater Bay Area.

**中文意图**：我的项目不是都属于同一小模块；PolyU 这几个课程能把机构、控制和规划连成一个完整机器人系统。不要把“课程名称相符”说成“已掌握课程内容”。

## 可能追问与英文答法

### Why not study only control engineering?

> Control is a major part of my interest, but the projects have shown me that control depends on the mechanism, state estimate, motion plan and actuator interface. I want training that treats the robot as an integrated system, while allowing me to deepen my control knowledge.

### Which course interests you most, and why?

> Advanced Control Technology is my first choice because I have used PID, LQR and VMC on different robots and want to understand their assumptions and design trade-offs more deeply. Robot Motion Planning is also important to me because my navigation project used a planner and a tracking controller, and I want to study how to connect them more systematically.

### What would you like to study in a dissertation?

> I am interested in a controlled study of balance under predictable centre-of-mass changes, or robust trajectory tracking for a mobile robot. I would first define a measurable question, use a matched model and test conditions, and compare tracking error, control effort and saturation events. I would refine the topic with a supervisor and the available equipment.

避免说自己已经完成了论文研究；CV/PS 明确表示结构化研究是接下来要发展的能力。

### Why is a mechanical-engineering robotics programme suitable for a CS student?

> My computer-science background helps me implement algorithms, but the robot’s behaviour also depends on geometry, dynamics, sensors and actuation. My experience with a closed-chain leg and a balancing robot made that gap very concrete. I want to learn enough mechanical and control principles to design software that respects the physical system.

### What will you contribute to the cohort?

> I can contribute practical questions from embedded control, mobile navigation and robot-platform integration. I have worked with both algorithms and the interfaces that connect them to hardware. I also know when I need to ask for help and verify an assumption instead of presenting a guess as a result.

## 建议反问

- How do students choose between the coursework and dissertation routes, and how are dissertation topics matched with supervisors?
- Are the robotics courses mainly lecture-based, project-based, or a combination of both in my intake year?
- What kinds of robot platforms or experimental resources are normally available for the dissertation?

## 这所学校的三条事实边界

1. 说清当前官网显示的 **Sept 2027 Entry**；如果系统写的是其他批次，以系统为准并先处理不一致。
2. 官网课程名称可以用于说明兴趣，但不要声称已被安排进入某课程或某研究组。
3. dissertation 方向只是初步兴趣；不要承诺还未定义的结果、指标或论文发表。
