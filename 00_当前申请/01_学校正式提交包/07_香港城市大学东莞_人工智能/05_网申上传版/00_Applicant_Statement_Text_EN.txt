I wish to pursue the MSc in Artificial Intelligence because my robotics projects have shown me that intelligent robots need more than reliable control: they need perception and decision systems that can learn from data and operate safely on physical hardware. I am completing a BEng in Computer Science and Technology at Shenzhen Technology University, where I rank 6th among 156 students.

I built an OpenHarmony smart-gardening system integrating INT8-quantised YOLOv5 inference on an RK3588 edge device, a LangGraph decision chain with a rule-based fast path for extreme states, and an irrigation state machine on a Hi3861. My internships and laboratory projects in ballbot balance control, mobile-robot navigation and multi-robot software further taught me to evaluate algorithms under real-world constraints.

Through the MSc in Artificial Intelligence, I hope to strengthen my foundations in machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning and reliable AI system design. I expect to apply this knowledge in a focused project on learning-based perception, state estimation or control for mobile robots. After graduation, I plan to work in the Greater Bay Area as a robotics algorithm engineer, developing perception, navigation and learning-based control for intelligent robots.
