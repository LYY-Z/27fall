I wish to pursue the MSc in Computer Science because my work in robotics has shown me that reliable intelligent robots depend not only on control algorithms, but also on sound software, data, and distributed-system foundations. I am completing a BEng in Computer Science and Technology at Shenzhen Technology University, where I rank 6th among 156 students in my major.

At Shenzhen Kaihong Digital Industry Development, I have worked on an OpenHarmony-based multi-robot platform, developing C++ device abstractions, robot-state management, and M-DDS coordination and safe task execution. Earlier internships at AIRS and Shenzhen Future Power gave me practical experience in ballbot balance control and dual-arm robot software. These projects have helped me connect computer science fundamentals with real robot deployment and have motivated me to build reusable, dependable software for intelligent robot platforms.

Through the programme, I hope to strengthen my software engineering foundations and develop systematic knowledge in artificial intelligence, data science, networking, and information security. I expect to learn rigorous methods for designing, evaluating, and deploying robust systems, and to apply them in a focused project related to robot-platform software, perception, or motion algorithms. After graduation, I plan to work in the Greater Bay Area on software for robot platforms and motion algorithms, with the longer-term goal of leading software development for a robotics product.
