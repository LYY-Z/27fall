# P2 连续追问模拟

训练方式：先不看答案口述，再看下面参考。每轮约10分钟，记录哪一步从理解变成背词。

**初学者练法：**第一遍只用中文讲清楚因果，不要求马上说出所有术语；卡住时停下来画对象、箭头和信号单位。第二遍再回答英文追问。先选题目涉及的那条支线：VR仿真、主从手臂或底盘。用普通话讲输入怎样变成输出，再用英文术语表达；不要把分支拼成一个没有证据的运行程序。先用[项目白话总览](../00_项目全貌与学习路线/03_P2端到端流程与面试地图.md)把主线复述一遍。

## Round 1

**Interviewer:** Why use relative VR control?

**Candidate:** Relative control starts from the current end-effector pose and applies controller motion from activation. It avoids a discontinuous jump to an unrelated VR coordinate origin.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What happens when the trigger is released and pressed again?

**回答依据：** 按键初始手柄/末端建立参考，不突然跳到VR绝对位置。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 2

**Interviewer:** How do you align VR and robot coordinates?

**Candidate:** I transform displacement with the alignment rotation and express the rotation increment through conjugation. The alignment matrix must represent a proper rotation, with orthogonal axes and determinant one.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Why would a reflection be invalid?

**回答依据：** 位置RaΔp，旋转RaΔRRaT，正交det1。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 3

**Interviewer:** What problem does Mink solve here?

**Candidate:** It solves a differential IK problem combining frame and posture tasks, returns a joint velocity and integrates over the timestep. It does not directly supply an arbitrary full motion trajectory.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What is the role of the current configuration?

## Round 4

**Interviewer:** What does a frame task specify?

**Candidate:** A frame task specifies a desired end-effector pose. Position and orientation costs determine their relative contribution to the optimization; a weighted task is not automatically a hard equality constraint.

**Interviewer, deeper:** How do you compare meters and radians?

**回答依据：** 末端SE3位置/朝向目标，softcost权重。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 5

**Interviewer:** Are task weights equivalent to joint limits?

**Candidate:** No. Weights trade off soft task errors. Joint-position, velocity and collision limits must be represented by the corresponding constraints or checks. Damping does not guarantee a hard bound.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Can the optimizer sacrifice a low-weight task?

**回答依据：** soft惩罚和hard不等式不同；lm_damping非限速。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 6

**Interviewer:** Why does damping reduce singular amplification?

**Candidate:** For a small nonzero singular value, damping replaces the reciprocal response with sigma over sigma squared plus lambda squared. It reduces large joint rates while leaving task error. At an exactly zero singular value, the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse already assigns zero, so that direction remains unachievable.

**Interviewer, deeper:** How would you choose or schedule damping?

**回答依据：** 区分小非零σ的放大与σ=0的伪逆取零；教学λ²、任务LM与全局damping也要区分。追问答法：先固定任务尺度，比较速度峰值与任务残差，再考虑阻尼安排；实际最优参数需要日志，不能报通用数值当项目结果。

## Round 7

**Interviewer:** Does holding the current state mean IK succeeded?

**Candidate:** Holding the current joint target is a failure behavior, not successful target tracking. A downstream position controller may continue applying torque to hold that target, so it is also different from disabling the actuator.

**Interviewer, deeper:** How would the system respond to a prolonged failure?

**回答依据：** 异常返回q，不是任务完成；保持仍PD出力。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 8

**Interviewer:** How does KDL compute gravity here?

**Candidate:** The source parses the URDF, extracts a joint chain and uses KDL's dynamics solver to calculate gravity torque from joint positions. Correct mass, CoM, joint axes and ordering are essential.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What happens if the chain endpoints are wrong?

**回答依据：** parse tree chain→ChainDynParam→JntToGravity(q)。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Contribution challenge

**Interviewer:** Which part did you personally implement, and what evidence supports that?

**Candidate template:** My own contribution was [verified module and decision]. The available source supports [specific function or interface]. A separate teaching experiment helps me understand the principle, but I do not use it as evidence of historical deployment.

## Evidence challenge

**Interviewer:** Did the available version actually run on the robot?

**Candidate:** The source snapshot and the deployed version must be matched using a commit, configuration and execution record. I would distinguish what I can trace in this snapshot from what I can substantiate with my own project records.

## 复盘答案标准

每问记录：公式/单位是否正确，能否指出函数，个人动作是否明确，验证是否有数据，限制是否诚实，英文是否可理解。不会则明确不知道的部分并解释已知部分。下次优先补一项，不改写成“已掌握”。
