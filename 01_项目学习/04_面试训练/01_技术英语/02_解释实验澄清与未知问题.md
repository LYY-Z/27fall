# 解释实验、请求澄清与处理未知问题

## 一个答案的五步

问题→输入输出→方法理由→本人实现→验证与限制。先说30秒重点，等追问再展开。Example: “The goal was to reject repeated commands. I used an action identifier and stored the result. The important limitation is that in-memory records do not survive a restart.”

## 三类不知道

|情况|可用句|随后要做什么|
|---|---|---|
|没听清|Could you repeat the last part, please?|只请求重复不盲答|
|范围不明|Are you asking about the mathematical model or the deployed implementation?|确认问题对象|
|忘记推导|I do not remember the full derivation, but I can explain the assumptions and the key steps.|写已知小例子，不能编公式|
|没做过该模块|I did not implement that module personally. My responsibility was …|说明实际模块|
|无性能记录|I do not have a verified measurement for that claim. I would evaluate it by …|讲指标、基线与条件|
|源码版本缺失|That historical version is not in the snapshot I currently have.|说明需要的commit/config/log|

## 怎样讲一个有效实验

“I fixed the model, initial state, time step and actuator limit. I changed only [variable]. I recorded [metric] and compared it against [baseline]. The result was [actual observation]. The limitation was [condition].”

没有运行就改为“I would…”，不要“I measured…”。教学结果加：“This was a separate learning simulation, not evidence of the historical robot deployment.”

## 把空泛表达改具体

“I used advanced control” → “The angle loop generated a rate reference, and the gyro-based rate loop produced the motor command.”

“It was safer” → “A stale command was rejected after the defined timeout in the implementation I checked.” 若源码没此检查，不能这样说。

“AI controlled the pump” → “The model produced a proposal; the execution layer checked the current mode and allowed commands before dispatch.”

## 技术选择要有取舍

PID容易建立基线，但多状态耦合不直观；LQR系统地权衡状态和输入，但依赖线性模型；MPC显式约束但需计算时间和失败处理；规则稳定可解释但覆盖有限；模型能处理语义但需输出校验。

不要说“X is always better”。说：“For this operating range, I chose X because … The trade-off was …”

## 简短研究提案

问题：腿长变化时固定增益的性能如何变化？假设：按腿长调度在多个局部模型改善误差，但快速切换可能产生瞬态。方法：同扰动/限幅下比较固定与调度，记录角误差、输入峰值、恢复时间和失效范围。限制：局部模型和仿真不能证明整段实机稳定。

英语：“I would compare fixed and scheduled gains under the same disturbances and limits. My hypothesis is that scheduling improves performance across leg lengths, but I would also check transition behavior and the validity of the local models.”

## 每日复盘

记原问题、自己的录音文字、具体错误、正确依据、下一次更短回答。技术没理解先补中文手算；英文不流畅再改句子。不要用背熟速度代替理解。
