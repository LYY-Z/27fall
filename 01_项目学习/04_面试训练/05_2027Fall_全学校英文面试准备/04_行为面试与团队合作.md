# 行为面试、团队合作与个人素养题库

> 面试官通过这些问题判断你是否真实、可靠、能合作、能学习。回答不需要“英雄故事”；最好说明当时目标、你的责任、做法、结果和反思。

## 1. STAR 的小白版本

- **S — Situation**：当时是什么项目/情境？一句话即可。
- **T — Task**：你本人要负责什么？
- **A — Action**：你实际采取了哪 2–3 个动作？
- **R — Result / Reflection**：发生了什么？你学到了什么？

四句英语框架：

> The situation was **[context]**. My responsibility was **[your task]**. I **[action one]** and **[action two]**. The result was **[verified result]**, and I learned **[lesson]**.

`Result` 可以是“问题更容易定位”“完成一次验证”或“明确了下一步”，不一定要是数字提升。

## 2. 有简历依据的行为案例

### 案例 A：跨模块排查导航状态不连续（P5）

**可用前提**：你的 CV 写明处理过时间、坐标系和运动方向不一致导致的漂移/位姿跳变，并输出连续车辆状态。把实际本人动作与团队工作核对后使用。

> In the mobile-robot project, the localization output sometimes drifted or jumped. My task was to help trace the issue across the sensor and state-estimation chain. I checked the timing, coordinate frames and motion direction of the IMU, wheel and LiDAR data, rather than changing the estimator parameters immediately. After aligning the inconsistent inputs, the system produced a more continuous vehicle state for planning and control. I learned that a localization problem may come from data conventions or timing, not only from the filter equation. I would report numerical accuracy only if I had a matched evaluation log.

**中文逻辑**：不是“我把 EKF 调好了”这种空话，而是先查数据时间、坐标方向，再调整/统一输入；结果说连续状态，不编精度值。

### 案例 B：把机器人任务执行分成提议、检查和执行（P6）

**可用前提**：CV 里写到 action-proposal、policy-check、execution、state-feedback chain。只说自己负责实现/集成的那一层，不扩写成正式安全认证。

> On the multi-robot platform, a high-level task should not directly bypass the device checks and move an actuator. My work included an execution chain in which a task proposal was checked against policy before execution, followed by state feedback. This made the system boundary clearer: a proposed action is not automatically an authorized action. It also reminded me to treat errors, stale state and device limits as part of the interface. I would not call the chain formally verified unless there were formal verification evidence.

### 案例 C：竞赛团队中的模块责任（P3）

**可用前提**：简历写明你在大学队伍负责云台、发射、底盘控制回路；比赛奖项属于队伍成绩。

> In the RoboMaster team, I was responsible for the gimbal, launcher and chassis control loops. The robot had to integrate those modules with the embedded platform and CAN feedback. I focused on making my interfaces and control behaviours understandable to the rest of the team and on testing changes with the integrated system. The team reached the 2025 national final and won third prize. I describe that as a team result, not an individual award.

**如果追问“你具体怎样沟通”**：加上一个真实的代码评审、接口对齐或比赛调试实例；如果没有具体记录，不要编造争论。

## 3. 高频人文问题：可直接练习的答案

### Tell me about a time you worked in a team.

> I have worked in both a competition team and multi-person robot projects. I try to make my own module’s inputs, outputs and assumptions clear, because integration depends on shared interfaces. In the RoboMaster project, I was responsible for several control loops, while the final robot and competition result depended on the whole team. I would give credit to the team for the result and explain my own contribution separately.

### How do you handle disagreement with a teammate?

> I would first make sure we are discussing the same objective and constraints. Then I would write down the assumptions behind each option and, where possible, compare them with a small test or a review of the evidence. If the disagreement remains, I would ask the project lead to decide based on the system requirements. I would support the final decision and document any remaining risk.

**准备要求**：面试官可能追问“给一个具体冲突”。你现有 CV/PS 没有记录具体冲突事件。面试前应从真实项目中挑一个发生过的例子；没有就诚实说“我暂时没有一个重大冲突案例”，再讲通常的处理流程。不要编造同事、对话或争吵。

### What do you do when a team member is behind schedule?

> I would first understand the blocker and check whether the task depends on an interface or resource that is missing. I would agree on a smaller deliverable and a realistic checkpoint, then tell the team if the schedule needs to change. The goal is to surface risk early and help the team finish the shared objective, not to blame someone.

### What is your role when you are not the leader?

> I focus on completing my responsibility, communicating progress and making my assumptions visible. If I notice a risk outside my module, I raise it with the relevant teammate instead of silently changing their component. I believe clear ownership and timely communication are both important.

### Tell me about a mistake or failure.

有证据的导航案例可以这样答：

> In a mobile-robot project, I learned that a state-estimation issue can come from inconsistent timing, frames or motion direction. My first instinct could have been to tune the filter, but I learned to verify the input conventions first. I now try to check timestamps, frames and units before attributing a symptom to the algorithm. I would need the matching test record before describing the exact impact numerically.

如果被问“这是你犯的错吗”，不能把团队系统问题伪装成你个人错误。可以说：

> This was a system issue that I helped investigate. I do not want to present it as a personal mistake if that would misstate what happened. The lesson I personally took from it was to check data conventions before changing algorithm parameters.

### How do you respond to critical feedback?

> I try to understand the specific behaviour the feedback refers to, rather than defending my first version. I ask what evidence or requirement was not met, then make a small change that can be reviewed. If I disagree, I explain the reason and propose a test. I also follow up to confirm whether the change solved the concern.

### How do you learn a topic you do not know?

> I start by identifying the inputs, outputs and assumptions, then work through one simple example by hand. I compare it with an implementation or a small experiment and record where my explanation stops matching the evidence. When I ask for help, I bring a specific question rather than only saying that I do not understand the whole topic.

### How do you manage pressure and deadlines?

> I break the work into a minimum working path and a verification path. I identify dependencies early, keep a short list of risks and communicate if a deadline is at risk. In hardware projects, I also reserve time for integration and safe testing because a module that works alone may still fail in the complete system.

### What is a weakness you are working on?

> I am working to improve the depth of my theoretical reasoning. In some earlier work, I focused first on implementation. I now spend more time identifying assumptions, units, limits and failure conditions before drawing conclusions. I am addressing this through structured study and small reproducible experiments.

### What value would you bring to our student community?

> I can bring practical experience from control, navigation, edge AI and robot-platform software. I am comfortable discussing both algorithm behaviour and integration constraints. I also value collaborative learning: I can share what I know while being open about what I still need to learn.

## 4. 安全、隐私与责任问题

### Should an AI agent be allowed to control a robot directly?

> I would not treat a language-model or agent proposal as an actuator command. The proposal should pass through explicit permission checks, state-freshness checks, capability checks and hardware limits. The system should have a defined fallback when inputs are stale, invalid or outside the allowed range, and it should record why an action was accepted or rejected.

### What would you do if your test could damage hardware?

> I would not run it without a safe test plan. I would start with a model or simulation, inspect limits, use reduced output or a constrained test mode where appropriate, and define an emergency stop or recovery procedure with the responsible supervisor. Hardware testing should have a clear risk boundary.

### How do you treat private or sensitive data?

> I would collect only what the task requires, restrict access, avoid exposing identifiers in logs and follow the project’s data-handling requirements. For robots that record audio, images or locations, consent and storage policies should be explicit.

## 5. 面试官追问“你个人贡献”的标准回答

> My documented contribution was **[specific module]**. I implemented or integrated **[specific action]**. The team or platform provided **[other components]**. I can support my contribution with **[code version, review, test record or report]**. I would not claim ownership of the complete system unless that is accurate.

如没有可展示的提交或日志，不要临场编 commit hash、实验表或个人负责范围。

## 6. 行为题追问：讲行动和证据，不演“完美人设”

### Q: Tell me about a disagreement in a team.

> I would first make sure we agree on the goal and constraints. Then I would compare options using a small test or a written trade-off table instead of arguing from preference. Once we decide, I would record the owner and interface so the team can move forward. For a real example, I would use [真实事件] and explain what I personally did.

### Q: Tell me about a failure.

> I would describe the observable failure, not label the whole project a failure. I would explain what evidence we collected, how we narrowed down the cause, what changed, and what remained unresolved. I would only claim a fix if we tested it again under the same conditions.

### Q: What if a teammate is behind schedule?

> I would check whether the blocker is unclear scope, a technical issue, or workload. I would make the next deliverable smaller and explicit, agree on an owner and check-in time, and tell the team early if integration is at risk. I would avoid silently taking over work without telling the team.

### Q: How do you handle safety or privacy concerns?

> I would identify what the system can affect, what data it collects, and what happens when perception or communication fails. I would add a clear safe state, limit access to sensitive data, and test failure cases instead of evaluating only the ideal path. The safeguards should match the deployment context.

### 真实故事筛选

- P5 可用于讲状态估计/规划链路里发现不一致并按数据源和时间戳排查；只讲材料能支持的细节。
- P6 可用于讲接口定义、设备状态和安全执行链的协作；准确区分个人与团队工作。
- P3 可用于讲控制、通信和硬件联调；只讲确实参与的事件。
- 没有真实冲突就用情景题回答，不编戏剧化经历。每个 STAR 故事准备：约束、个人动作、结果证据、复做时的改进。
