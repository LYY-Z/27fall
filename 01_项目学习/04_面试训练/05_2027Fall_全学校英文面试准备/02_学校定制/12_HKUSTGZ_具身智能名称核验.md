提交包的目录与 PS 把项目写作 **MSc in Embodied Intelligence, HKUST(GZ)**。本次查阅 HKUST(GZ) 官方研究生项目目录，没有找到这个名称的授课型 MSc；官方可核验的是 **Robotics and Autonomous Systems 的 MPhil/PhD**。HKUST(GZ) 研究型硕士统一入口和学域安排也与授课型 MSc 不同。[官方 ROAS MPhil/PhD 项目页](https://fytgs.hkust-gz.edu.cn/programs/robotics-and-autonomous-systems-3)｜[Systems Hub 项目介绍](https://sysh.hkust-gz.edu.cn/)｜[MPhil 项目入口说明](https://cft.hkust-gz.edu.cn/rbm-program/admission/hub-thrust/)

**在面试准备中不要把材料里的名字继续当作事实重复。**必须用网申系统、缴费记录/申请编号和面试邀请确认：学位类型、项目正式名称、申请入口、面试环节。若系统实际上对应其他高校/学位，必须重写这页。

## 若确认为 MPhil in Robotics and Autonomous Systems，可使用的动机答案

> I am interested in robotics and autonomous systems because my experience has moved from balance control and locomotion to navigation, manipulation and platform software. I want to study how perception, planning, control and learning can work together in a physical robot. My practical work has given me specific questions, such as how state uncertainty and hardware limits affect reliable motion. I would like to develop a focused research question, learn to design reproducible experiments and contribute to the Robotics and Autonomous Systems research community. I would first align my topic with a supervisor’s current work and the resources available in the programme.

## 研究型面试和授课型面试的区别

如果确认为 MPhil/PhD 研究型项目，面试重点不能只是“我喜欢机器人”或“课程很匹配”。必须准备：

1. 一个窄而具体的研究问题；
2. 为什么这个问题值得研究、已有方法哪里不足；
3. 你会先做什么基线、用什么平台/数据、测什么指标；
4. 哪些实验结果会支持/否定你的假设；
5. 你的数学/编程/实验准备，以及目前缺口；
6. 与具体导师研究方向的匹配证据。

## 可作为讨论起点的研究问题，不是已完成研究

### 候选方向 A：移动机器人中的估计不确定性

> I am interested in how localization uncertainty affects safe trajectory tracking near obstacles. A first study could compare a baseline controller with an uncertainty-aware strategy under repeatable localization noise and delay. I would measure tracking error, clearance, computation time and failure cases.

### 候选方向 B：腿式/轮腿机器人在形态变化下的控制

> I am interested in how leg-length changes affect the balance controller and virtual-force mapping. I would first build a validated model, define a limited set of leg configurations, and compare tracking and saturation behaviour across them.

### 候选方向 C：面向真实机器人的学习决策安全

> I am interested in how an AI or learned policy can propose actions while a separate safety layer enforces physical constraints. I would study a narrowly defined task, compare with a rule-based baseline and test both nominal and abnormal conditions.

这些只是基于已学项目形成的研究兴趣；面试前要读拟申请导师的具体论文，再决定用哪一项。不要把任何一项称为“我的已完成研究成果”。

## 可能追问与英文答法

### Why research rather than a taught master’s degree?

> I want to move beyond implementing a method and learn how to formulate a question, compare methods fairly and interpret evidence. My projects have given me practical problems, but I still need research training in literature review, experimental design and scientific writing. I would choose the research route only if the actual programme and supervision fit that goal.

### What is the first experiment you would run?

> I would begin with a small, reproducible baseline experiment. I would hold the platform, task, limits and disturbances constant, vary one design choice at a time, and log inputs, outputs, errors and failure events. I would use the result to refine the hypothesis rather than claiming success from a single run.

### What if your experiment disproves your hypothesis?

> That would still be useful evidence. I would check whether the setup and assumptions were valid, report the negative result and identify what it changes about the question. I would not adjust the metrics after seeing the result just to make the method look successful.

### Which faculty member do you want to work with?

> I am still verifying supervisor fit. I would name a faculty member only after reading their recent work and identifying a specific connection to my proposed question. I would explain that connection, rather than using a general statement that their research is interesting.

## 必须在面试前完成的核验

- 系统中的正式学位全名与项目编号；
- 研究生申请入口和具体学域/方向；
- 是否授课/研究型、是否需要研究计划或导师意向；
- 面试邀请是否由 HKUST(GZ) 官方地址发出、对应哪个申请编号；
- PS 是否把学校、学位类型和项目名称写对；
- 若确认为 ROAS MPhil，阅读至少 2–3 篇拟匹配导师的近期论文并准备逐篇摘要。
