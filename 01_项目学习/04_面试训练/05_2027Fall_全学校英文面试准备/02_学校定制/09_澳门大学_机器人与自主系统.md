## 45–60 秒：Why UM Robotics and Autonomous Systems?

> I am applying to the MSc in Robotics and Autonomous Systems because I want to connect the separate parts of my robotics experience into a more systematic view. My projects have covered balance control, legged locomotion, mobile-robot localization and planning, and dual-arm manipulation. The programme’s mobile-robotics and sensors-and-actuators foundation is relevant to the systems I have worked on, while its robotics-and-control and autonomous-systems electives match the areas I want to deepen. I am also interested in the project report, because I want to learn how to define a careful experiment and support engineering conclusions with evidence. My career goal is to work on robotics algorithms in the Greater Bay Area.

## 可能追问与英文答法

### Which of your projects would you use for a project report?

> I would consider a study on mobile-robot navigation or balance under a controlled change in the robot state. For example, I could examine how localization uncertainty affects trajectory tracking, or how an arm-induced centre-of-mass shift affects a balancing controller. I would first define a narrow question, identify the available platform and data, and agree on measurable outcomes with a supervisor.

### How do localization and control interact?

> The controller uses the estimated state, not the unknown true state. If the estimate has drift, delay or a frame mismatch, the controller may follow an incorrect reference even when its control law is implemented correctly. I would monitor uncertainty and data freshness, align timestamps and frames, and evaluate the complete localization-to-control chain.

### What is the value of a sensor-and-actuator course for a CS student?

> It helps me connect software values to physical measurements and actions. An encoder value needs scaling and a reference frame; a motor command has limits and a hardware interpretation. Understanding those details helps me design interfaces and algorithms that are meaningful on a real robot.

### How do you distinguish a demonstration from a reliable result?

> A demonstration shows that a system worked in a particular case. A reliable result requires defined conditions, repeatable trials, suitable metrics and records of failures or limits. I would report the test setup and uncertainty rather than generalizing from one successful run.

### Which area would you prioritize after graduation?

> I would initially focus on navigation and motion control for mobile or legged robots. I want to work on a clearly scoped module, learn the production and validation process, and then take on larger system responsibilities as my technical depth grows.
