### 9. What was a difficult problem in your work, and how did you approach it?

**中文题目：工作中遇到过什么困难，你是如何处理的？**

> One difficult problem is combining information that does not describe the same robot state. A wheel encoder, an IMU and an actuator report may use different timestamps or coordinate frames. If a controller treats them as one consistent snapshot, its behaviour can be misleading. I learned to check the data path first: where each value comes from, what frame and time it refers to, and whether it is still valid. In my platform work, I focused on making these definitions explicit for the layers above. That experience taught me to diagnose upstream data before changing an algorithm.

**中文翻译**

一个困难的问题是，如何组合那些并不描述同一个机器人状态的信息。轮子编码器、IMU和执行器反馈，可能使用不同时间戳或坐标系。如果控制器把它们当成同一时刻的一致状态快照，结果就可能产生误导。我学会先检查数据链：每个值来自哪里、对应什么坐标系和时间，以及是否仍然有效。在平台工作中，我重点让这些定义对上层模块更加明确。这段经历让我学会在修改算法之前，先诊断上游数据问题。

> I first ask what problem the tool solves and what its inputs and outputs mean. Then I work through a small example, check the assumptions and connect it to the larger system. When I used robotics tools for modelling or inverse kinematics, understanding coordinate frames and state variables mattered more than memorising an API call. I also document the result so I can explain it to someone else. This approach helps me use a tool deliberately rather than treating it as a black box.

**中文翻译**

我首先会问：这个工具解决什么问题，它的输入和输出分别意味着什么？然后我会做一个小例子，核对假设，并把它放回更大的系统中理解。在使用机器人建模或逆运动学工具时，理解坐标系和状态变量，比记住某个API调用更重要。我也会记录结果，使自己能够向别人解释。这种方式帮助我有意识地使用工具，而不是只把它当作黑盒。

## 5. Teamwork, pressure and personal qualities

*五、团队合作、压力与个人特点*

### 14. How do you work with a team?

**中文题目：你如何与团队合作？**

> I try to make the interface between people’s work as clear as the interface between software modules. In a robot project, one person may own a controller while another owns perception or communication. We need to agree on the meaning, units and timing of shared data, integrate early and report blockers promptly. In the RoboMaster team, my responsibility was the gimbal, launcher and chassis control loops, while the robot’s competition result belonged to the whole team. That experience taught me to be accountable for my part and to support the full system.

**中文翻译**

我会尽量让不同成员之间的工作衔接，像软件模块之间的接口一样清楚。在机器人项目中，一名成员可能负责控制器，另一名成员负责感知或通信。我们需要对共享数据的含义、单位和时间达成一致，尽早集成，并及时报告阻碍进度的问题。在RoboMaster团队中，我负责云台、发射机构和底盘控制环，而机器人的比赛成绩属于整个团队。这段经历让我学会对自己的部分负责，同时支持完整系统。

### 15. How would you handle a disagreement with a teammate?

**中文题目：你会如何处理与队友的分歧？**

> I would begin by checking whether we agree on the goal and constraints. Technical disagreements often come from different assumptions about data, timing or the cost of failure. I would listen to the other person’s reasoning, explain mine and, where possible, compare the options with a small test or a clear criterion. Once a decision is made, I would document it and help implement it. I care more about reaching a dependable result than defending my first idea.

**中文翻译**

我会先确认大家是否认同同一个目标和约束。技术分歧经常来自对数据、时间或失效代价的不同假设。我会听取对方的理由，解释自己的判断，并在可行时通过一个小测试或清晰的标准比较方案。作出决定之后，我会记录结论并协助实施。相比维护自己的第一个想法，我更关心团队能否得到可靠的结果。

**This is a method answer. If asked for a past conflict, use an actual event you can describe; do not invent one.**

**中文说明**

**这是对处理方法的回答。**如果问题要求讲过去的一次冲突，应当使用自己能描述清楚的真实经历。

### 16. How do you manage pressure or several deadlines?

**中文题目：你如何应对压力或多个截止日期？**

> I identify which tasks block other work and which failures would be most costly. I then break the work into small, checkable steps and communicate progress and risks early. In a robot system, I would prioritise valid feedback and safe execution before fine performance tuning. This makes the work more manageable and gives the team a clear basis for decisions. Under pressure, I try to simplify the plan without losing the essential checks.

**中文翻译**

我会先找出哪些任务会阻碍其他工作，以及哪些失误的代价最高。随后，我把工作拆成较小、可以检查的步骤，并尽早沟通进度与风险。在机器人系统中，我会优先保证反馈有效、执行安全，再进一步调整性能。这让工作更容易管理，也为团队决策提供清楚的依据。压力较大时，我会简化计划，同时保留关键检查。

### 17. What is your greatest strength?

**中文题目：你最大的优势是什么？**

> My strength is connecting several layers of a technical system. I have worked with control algorithms, robot-state data, device interfaces and hardware execution, so I tend to ask how one layer affects the next. For example, when a robot behaves unexpectedly, I do not immediately assume the control law is wrong; I also check feedback, timing and command handling. This habit helps me find practical causes of problems and work effectively with teammates who own different modules.

**中文翻译**

我的优势是能够把技术系统的多个层面联系起来。我做过控制算法、机器人状态数据、设备接口和硬件执行方面的工作，所以会关注一个层面如何影响下一个层面。例如，机器人表现异常时，我不会马上认定是控制规律有问题，也会检查反馈、时间和命令处理。这种习惯有助于我找到实际原因，并与负责不同模块的成员有效合作。

### 18. What is an area you still need to improve?

**中文翻译**

我希望更系统地理解信息安全和分布式软件设计。在平台工作中，设备需要共享状态并接收任务命令，因此我接触过这两个领域。但仅靠项目经验，我还没有建立起完整的系统设计与评估框架。我一直通过阅读实现细节、追踪完整命令流程来学习。在硕士阶段，我希望用更扎实的原理和方法，把这些实践认识连接起来。

**中文说明**

**这段回答与个人陈述一致。**它说明真实的学习目标，无需套用“过于追求完美”等常见模板。

## 6. Broader open-ended questions

*六、其他开放式问题*

### 20. How do you think AI will change software engineering?

**中文题目：你认为人工智能会怎样改变软件工程？**

> AI tools can help developers explore ideas, write routine code and inspect large amounts of information more quickly. But software engineers still have to define the problem, check the assumptions and verify what the system actually does. This matters especially in robotics, where an incorrect output can become a physical action. I see AI as a useful assistant within a well-designed workflow, with testing, human judgement and clear responsibility for the final system.

**中文翻译**

人工智能工具可以帮助开发者更快地探索想法、编写常规代码和检查大量信息。但软件工程师仍然需要定义问题、核对假设，并验证系统实际做了什么。这在机器人领域尤其重要，因为错误输出可能变成物理动作。我把人工智能视为一个有良好流程支持的助手，同时需要测试、人的判断，以及对最终系统明确的责任。

### 21. What does responsible technology development mean to you?

**中文题目：你如何理解负责任的技术开发？**

> It means thinking about the effect of a system after it leaves the developer’s screen. For a robot platform, that includes whether the input data is fresh, whether the requested action is allowed, and whether feedback confirms what happened. The same principle applies to privacy and security: access and failure behaviour should be designed, not assumed. I would rather report a limitation honestly and improve it than present an unverified result as reliable.

**中文翻译**

这意味着思考系统离开开发者屏幕之后会产生什么影响。对于机器人平台，需要考虑输入数据是否新鲜、请求的动作是否被允许，以及反馈能否确认实际发生的事情。同样的原则也适用于隐私与安全：访问权限和失效时的行为应当经过设计，不能想当然。我宁愿如实说明某个局限并加以改进，也不把未经验证的结果说成可靠结果。

### 22. Why should we admit you?

**中文题目：我们为什么应该录取你？**

> I have a clear reason for choosing this programme and a realistic plan for using it. My computer-science education gives me a base, while my robotics projects have shown me the software and data problems I want to understand more deeply. I have worked on real systems, learned from integration difficulties and taken responsibility for specific modules. I would bring that practical experience and a willingness to learn to the programme, then apply the training to reliable robot-platform software.

**中文翻译**

我有明确的申请理由，也有利用这个项目学习的现实计划。计算机科学本科教育给了我基础，而机器人项目让我发现了希望更深入理解的软件和数据问题。我做过真实系统，从集成困难中学习，也承担过具体模块的责任。我会把这些实践经验和主动学习的态度带进项目，再将所学应用于可靠的机器人平台软件。
