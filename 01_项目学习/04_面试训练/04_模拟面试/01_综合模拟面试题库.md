## 3. 白板A：雅可比与对偶力映射

给J=[[-1,-1],[1,0]]，qdot=[.1,.2]rad/s。末端速度Jqdot=[−.3,.1]m/s。给F=[0,10]N，τ=JᵀF=[10,0]N·m；功率F·v=1W，τ·qdot=1W。

**Interviewer:** Why is the transpose used for force mapping?

**Candidate:** Substituting the velocity relation into power gives equal task and joint power for every joint velocity. That requires the transpose mapping. The pseudoinverse solves a different problem: recovering joint velocity from a task-velocity target.

追问：如果两列线性相关会怎样？答：可实现任务速度的独立方向减少，小非零奇异值反解会放大关节速度；阻尼减少放大但有残差。

## 4. 白板B：Kalman不是随意平均

预测10m、测量12m、P⁻4m²、R1m²，直接测位置H=1。k=.8，创新2m，结果11.6m；更新方差.8m²。方差不等于本次真实误差，也不是置信度百分数。

**Interviewer:** Where does that weight come from?

**Candidate:** Under zero-mean, uncorrelated errors, the fused-error variance is (one minus k) squared times the prediction variance, plus k squared times the measurement variance. Minimizing it gives k equal to P divided by P plus R.

追问：仅测位置为何能改速度？答：预测位置和速度误差相关，PHᵀ包含速度相关项；看[KF矩阵推导](../../01_通用基础/04_传感器与状态估计/05_Kalman矩阵协方差与创新.md)。

## 5. 白板C：LQR、MPC与实际输入

简化x_next=x+u，下一误差权4、输入权1，J=4(x+u)²+u²；完成平方得到u=−.8x。x=1、输入界±.2时不能执行−.8；一阶段带界优化最优为−.2。完整LQR还考虑更长未来和模型；完整MPC优化序列，每步只执行第一项。

**Interviewer:** Why does MPC need the previous applied input?

**Candidate:** The first rate constraint is measured from the input actually applied in the previous cycle. A fallback command must therefore become the next cycle’s starting input. A planned input that was never executed is the wrong reference.

追问：限幅后的LQR仍保证原最优吗？答：不能；无约束最优推导不覆盖饱和，需要分析约束与闭环范围。

## 6. 跨项目比较

1. **P1/P7 versus P5:** Why does balance need a faster inner response than navigation? 答：身体失稳时间尺度与路径位置变化不同，但真实带宽受机构/增益/延迟影响，不能仅比较配置频率。
2. **P2 versus P6:** What makes an asynchronous command safe to keep after the caller returns? 答：值/拥有型捕获保证数据寿命；状态同步、取消、时效和实际执行反馈另有合同。
3. **P4 versus P5:** Why separate confidence from freshness? 答：高confidence的旧观测仍可能与当前状态不符；时间、坐标和模型必须对齐。
4. **P3 versus P7:** Why are motor limits part of the explanation? 答：限制后实际动作/身体力可能偏离期望；不只报告控制器原始输出。

## 7. 贡献与验证挑战

**Interviewer:** Which part did you implement yourself?

**Candidate template:** My verified contribution was [module and decision]. The corresponding evidence is [version, change or record]. Other modules were team work. I use the new teaching example to explain the principle, rather than as evidence of historical deployment.

**Interviewer:** What did you measure, and what is still uncertain?

**Candidate template:** I measured [actual metric under actual conditions]. I distinguish that result from the configured rate and model prediction. The remaining limitation is [supported gap].

若没有日志：I can trace the source behavior, but I do not have a matched execution record for that claim. I would evaluate it with time-aligned inputs, outputs, errors and limit events.
