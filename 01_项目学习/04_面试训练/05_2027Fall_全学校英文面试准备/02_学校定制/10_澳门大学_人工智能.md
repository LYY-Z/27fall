## 45–60 秒：Why UM AI?

> I am applying to the MSc in Artificial Intelligence because my experience with AI has been practical, and I want a stronger understanding of the mathematics and learning methods behind it. In my smart-gardening project, I built a YOLOv5 edge-inference chain and connected the result to a decision process and an irrigation controller. That work raised questions about confidence, evaluation and safe use of a model. My robotics projects also showed me how perception and state estimation affect downstream planning and control. I am interested in the programme’s machine-learning, mathematics, deep-learning and computer-vision training, and I hope to apply it to reliable AI components for robots.

## 面试主线：从“能部署”走到“能判断方法是否合适”

你已有的基础：P4 的视觉推理、INT8 部署、LangGraph 决策链、Hi3861 泵控制；P5 的状态估计与规划；P6 的策略检查与执行反馈。

还要补强的方向：机器学习数学、数据质量、模型评估、泛化与不确定性。承认这些缺口比声称“我已经精通 AI”更可信。

## 可能追问与英文答法

### What have you actually done with machine learning?

> My documented work includes building and deploying a YOLOv5 inference pipeline on an RK3588 device and connecting its structured outputs to a decision and irrigation system. I can explain the deployment and integration chain. I would distinguish that from model training, and I would only claim training work if I can describe the dataset, training setup and changes I personally made.

### How would you evaluate an AI model for robotics?

> I would evaluate the model on representative data using task-specific metrics, not only a single overall score. I would check confidence calibration, latency, memory use and behaviour under poor, delayed or missing observations. Then I would test how the model affects the complete robot task, including safety checks and downstream control.

### Why is mathematics important for AI?

> Linear algebra, probability, statistics and optimization help explain how models represent data, learn parameters and behave under uncertainty. I have used AI tools in applications, but I want to understand these foundations so I can select methods, interpret results and diagnose failures more systematically.

### Would you replace a classical robot controller with a learned policy?

> Not automatically. A learned policy may help with a task that is difficult to model, but I would compare it with a model-based baseline and define safety constraints. I would first test it in a controlled setting, monitor its inputs and outputs, and keep a fallback or independent safety layer where appropriate.

### What is your longer-term technical interest?

> I am interested in combining perception and learning with robot navigation and control. I want to understand when learning improves a system, how to measure that improvement and how to keep the result dependable under hardware constraints.
