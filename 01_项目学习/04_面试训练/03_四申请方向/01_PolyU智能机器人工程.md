## 六个公共追问及参考答法

1. **Why this programme rather than only continuing to work?** 中文：项目经验发现理论缺口。English: “Practical work helps me identify problems, but I need systematic training in the models, assumptions and analysis behind the algorithms.” 追问：Name one limitation you encountered.
2. **What is your strongest project?** 中文：选本人职责证据最完整项目，先问题再动作。English: “I would choose [project] because my responsibility in [module] is specific. The key design decision was [decision], and I can support it with [record].” 方括号待本人填写。
3. **What do you still need to learn?** 中文：说具体知识缺口与补法。English: “I need a stronger foundation in [topic]. I am working through small derivations and separate learning experiments before generalizing them to a robot.” 不说已掌握全部。
4. **What research question would you investigate?** 中文：给可比较问题、假设和指标。English: “I would compare [methods] under the same model, limits and disturbances, measuring error, computational cost and failure conditions.”
5. **What is your career goal?** 中文：湾区机器人算法，把短期模块能力到长期完整算法栈。English: “I want to develop robotics algorithms in the Greater Bay Area, initially strengthening a specific area and eventually taking responsibility for a complete algorithm stack.”
6. **What if your source differs from the submitted description?** 中文：说明历史版本与现存版本，用记录补证。English: “I would match the deployed version using its configuration, commit and logs. I would not use a different source snapshot to imply the same deployment.”
