# 通用英文回答与自我介绍

> 先理解，再用自己的节奏说。每段英文后面有中文意图；不要把模板里没有的项目结果、职责或感受临场编进去。

## 1. 一句话定位自己

**English**

> I am a computer science student with hands-on experience in robot control, navigation and platform software. My goal is to make intelligent robot algorithms reliable on real hardware.

**中文意思**：我是计算机专业学生，做过机器人控制、导航和平台软件；想把智能算法做成能在真实硬件上可靠运行的系统。

这句话是面试主线。申请机器人项目时强调 control, navigation, manipulation；申请 CS 时强调 software, data, distributed systems；申请 AI 时强调 perception, learning, evaluation and safe deployment。

## 2. 30 秒自我介绍

> Hello, my name is Yongyi Lai. I am a final-year Computer Science and Technology student at Shenzhen Technology University. I am ranked sixth out of 156 students in my major, and I expect to graduate in June 2027. I have worked on robot balance, mobile navigation, dual-arm teleoperation and an OpenHarmony multi-robot platform. These experiences have made me interested in building reliable intelligent systems that connect algorithms with real hardware. I am now applying for postgraduate training to strengthen the theory behind my practical work.

**中文逻辑**：姓名/专业 → 学业情况 → 经历范围 → 为什么继续读研。时长约 25–35 秒。

## 3. 60 秒自我介绍：机器人/机器人与自主系统版

> Hello, my name is Yongyi Lai. I am a final-year Computer Science and Technology student at Shenzhen Technology University, with an average score of 87.63 out of 100 and a major ranking of sixth out of 156. I expect to graduate in June 2027.
>
> My main interest is robotics algorithms that work reliably on physical systems. At AIRS, I worked on balance control for a three-wheel ballbot carrying two arms. I developed a cascaded PID controller, used a simplified inverted-pendulum model and LQR for analysis, and estimated arm-related centre-of-mass changes for gravity feedforward. I also worked on a wheel-legged robot and a car-like mobile robot, covering LQR/VMC and the localisation-planning-MPC chain.
>
> My internships in dual-arm teleoperation and OpenHarmony robot platforms broadened my experience from control algorithms to software integration. I want to strengthen my foundations in modelling, estimation, planning and control, and develop into a robotics algorithm engineer in the Greater Bay Area.

**结构提示**：学业 10 秒；核心经历 30 秒；申请动机和职业方向 15 秒。不要在开场把所有工具名一次念完。

## 4. 60 秒自我介绍：计算机科学版

> Hello, my name is Yongyi Lai. I am a final-year Computer Science and Technology student at Shenzhen Technology University, ranked sixth out of 156 students in my major. My robotics work has taught me that an algorithm is only one part of a dependable robot. Data timestamps, coordinate frames, interfaces, concurrency and safe execution can be just as important.
>
> At Kaihong, I have been working on an OpenHarmony multi-robot platform. My work includes capability abstraction, mapping ROS and ROS 2 commands to lower-level drivers, synchronising robot state, and coordinating tasks through M-DDS. Earlier projects gave me experience with embedded control, navigation and edge AI. They showed me how strongly software architecture affects physical behaviour.
>
> I am applying for postgraduate study in Computer Science because I want systematic training in software, data and systems engineering. My short-term goal is to work on robot platform or robotics software, and later take responsibility for a reliable software stack for robot products.

**事实提醒**：如果实习在面试前已结束，把 “I have been working” 改为 “I worked”；具体时间按更新后的 CV。

## 5. 60 秒自我介绍：人工智能版

> Hello, my name is Yongyi Lai. I am a final-year Computer Science and Technology student at Shenzhen Technology University. My experience combines robot control with practical AI deployment.
>
> In a smart-gardening project, I built an edge-vision and irrigation loop using YOLOv5 on an RK3588 device, a LangGraph decision chain and a local pump state machine on a Hi3861 board. The project taught me that model accuracy alone is not enough: confidence, timestamps, rule-based safety checks and actuator behaviour all matter. In my robotics work, I have also seen how perception and state estimation affect planning and control on physical platforms.
>
> I have used AI tools in applications, but I want stronger foundations in machine learning, mathematics and evaluation. After the programme, I hope to develop robotics algorithms in the Greater Bay Area, connecting learning-based perception and decision-making with safe robot execution.

## 6. 90–120 秒综合自我介绍

> Hello, my name is Yongyi Lai. I am a final-year Computer Science and Technology student at Shenzhen Technology University. My average score is 87.63 out of 100, and I rank sixth out of 156 students in my major. I expect to graduate in June 2027.
>
> I became interested in robotics because I wanted to understand how software decisions produce physical behaviour. My first major experience was embedded control for a RoboMaster hero robot, where I worked on the gimbal, launcher and chassis control loops. Later, at AIRS, I worked on balance control for a ballbot with a dual-arm upper body. The control chain used IMU and wheel feedback, cascaded PID, kinematic allocation to the wheels and CAN motor execution. I also used a simplified model and LQR to analyse the relationship between state weights, control effort and saturation, and used a URDF-based model to estimate arm-related centre-of-mass changes.
>
> I then worked on a wheel-legged robot and a car-like navigation platform. These projects helped me study closed-chain kinematics, LQR/VMC, localisation, Hybrid A*, TEB and MPC. At Future Power, I worked on dual-arm VR teleoperation and leader-follower teaching. At Kaihong, my work shifted toward a multi-robot software platform on OpenHarmony, including device capabilities, state consistency and task execution safeguards. I also built an OpenHarmony smart-gardening project that connected edge vision to irrigation.
>
> Across these experiences, I have become more aware of my gaps. I want deeper training in [programme-specific topics], especially the mathematics and methods behind the tools I have used. My near-term goal is to work as a robotics [algorithm/software/AI] engineer in the Greater Bay Area. In the longer term, I hope to take responsibility for an integrated robot system, while continuing to learn from evidence and real-world testing.

最后一句中的 `[programme-specific topics]` 要换成：

- 机器人专业：“robot modelling, sensing, motion planning and control”
- 计算机专业：“algorithms, software engineering, data systems and AI”
- AI 专业：“machine-learning foundations, optimization, evaluation and trustworthy deployment”

## 7. “Tell me about yourself” 与 “Walk me through your CV” 的区别

- **Tell me about yourself**：讲一条主线和一个选择理由，不逐行念 CV。
- **Walk me through your CV**：按教育、实习、项目顺序概括；每段只挑与申请项目最相关的一件事。
- 两题都应以“现在申请什么、为什么这段经历能支撑它”收尾。

可用短句：

> My CV may look broad, but the common thread is the integration of algorithms with robot systems. I have moved from embedded control to navigation, manipulation and platform software.

中文：经历虽然跨方向，但主线是机器人算法与系统集成；路径从嵌入式控制扩展到导航、操作和平台软件。

## 8. “Why did you study Computer Science but choose robotics?”

> Computer Science gave me the tools to build software, reason about algorithms and work with data. Robotics gave me a concrete system in which those tools have to interact with mechanics, sensors and physical constraints. My projects showed me that I enjoy this intersection. I do not see robotics as a departure from computer science; I see it as a demanding application area that requires both strong software and engineering foundations.

**中文逻辑**：CS 是基础；机器人是应用交叉领域；这不是放弃 CS，而是把 CS 放进受物理约束的系统里。

## 9. “Why pursue a master’s degree now?”

> My practical experience has helped me identify specific questions, but it has not replaced systematic training. I have implemented controllers and robot software, yet I want a deeper understanding of modelling, optimization, machine learning and system design. Postgraduate study is the right next step because I can connect my hands-on experience to rigorous methods and learn to evaluate them more carefully.

如果追问“举一个具体缺口”：

> For example, I can use an optimizer or a state estimator in a project, but I want to understand its assumptions, numerical behaviour and failure conditions well enough to choose and validate it systematically.

## 10. “Why should we admit you?”

> I would bring three things. First, I have worked across control, navigation, manipulation and robot software, so I can connect ideas across layers. Second, I have seen the difference between a model-level result and what a physical robot can execute. Third, I am clear about what I still need to learn, and I am prepared to build that knowledge carefully. I would contribute practical questions and a collaborative engineering mindset to the programme.

不要说“我比其他申请者强”。重点是“我能带来什么 + 为什么课程适合 + 知道自己的缺口”。

## 11. 短期、长期职业规划

### 稳妥的通用回答

> In the first one to three years after graduation, I hope to work as a robotics algorithm engineer in the Greater Bay Area. I would like to deepen one or two areas—such as navigation, motion control or robot software—while learning how to test algorithms against real sensor, timing and actuator constraints. Over the longer term, I hope to become an engineer who can take responsibility for an integrated robot system, from perception and planning to execution and validation. I understand that this will require sustained technical learning and teamwork.

### 按申请方向调整

|申请方向|短期重点|长期方向|
|---|---|---|
|机器人/自主系统|定位、运动规划、控制与硬件验证|对机器人完整算法链负责|
|计算机科学|机器人平台、数据/软件工程、分布式系统|设计可复用、可靠的机器人软件平台|
|人工智能|学习、视觉、模型评估与安全部署|让学习算法与实体机器人可靠协作|
|具身智能研究型方向|先形成清楚研究问题、方法与实验能力|在机器人/具身智能方向开展有证据的研究或算法研发|

### 被问“你说的短期具体是什么”时

> By short term, I mean my first role after graduation and the first few years of development. I would begin with a clearly scoped module, learn the product and evaluation process, and gradually take responsibility for a larger part of the system.

这比承诺“几年内当负责人/创业/发表顶会”更可信。

## 12. 优点与需要改进的地方

### 优点

> One strength is that I tend to trace a problem across the full system instead of looking only at one algorithm. For example, a robot-control issue can come from a state estimate, a coordinate convention, a timing mismatch, a limit or the driver interface. I try to check the whole chain before drawing a conclusion.

### 需要改进的地方

> One area I am working to improve is the depth of my theoretical understanding. In the past, I sometimes focused first on making a system work. My project study has made me more disciplined about deriving assumptions, checking units and failure conditions, and separating source-code evidence from experimental evidence. I am addressing this through structured coursework and small, testable experiments.

要准备一个真实例子支持优点/改进点；不使用“我太完美主义”这类空泛弱点。

## 14. 高频动机问题：可复用回答框架

### Why robotics?

> Robotics combines software with the physical world. A robot has to estimate its state, make a decision and execute that decision through real hardware. I enjoy this complete engineering challenge, especially the gap between a clean algorithm and reliable behaviour on a real platform.

### Why AI?

> I have used computer vision and an agent-based decision chain in an application, but I have mainly worked on deployment and integration. I want to understand learning algorithms, their assumptions and evaluation more deeply, so I can decide when a learned component is useful and how to make its output safe in a physical system.

### Why Computer Science?

> My robot projects repeatedly showed that reliable behaviour depends on algorithms, interfaces, data, operating systems and software architecture. I want to strengthen these foundations so that robotics software can be more reliable and reusable across platforms.

### Why this school rather than another one?

> I am choosing this programme for its fit with [two verified curriculum elements]. My experience in [one project] has given me practical questions in those areas. I want to use the programme to build a stronger foundation in [specific gap], and then apply it to [career direction].

## 15. 不知道答案时，不要乱猜

> I am not fully sure, and I do not want to guess. My current understanding is [what I can explain]. I would verify [the uncertain assumption] and then test it by [method].

例如被问“你们实时频率是多少”，若只有代码里的配置值、没有测量日志：

> The configured interval is not the same as a measured control rate. I would check timestamped cycle logs and report the distribution, including the median, maximum and missed-cycle count.

这类回答展示边界意识，不等于回避问题。

## 16. 英语答题统一节奏

每个技术回答按四句走：

1. **Problem**：The system needed to…
2. **Input and method**：It used … and computed …
3. **My role / output**：My part was …; the output was …
4. **Check and limit**：We checked …; one limitation was …

例如：

> The ballbot needed to remain upright while its arms moved. The controller used IMU and wheel feedback, and the outer loop generated bounded attitude references for the inner loop. My work focused on the balance-control chain and on-robot tuning. We checked the behaviour under fixed arm trajectories, while I would avoid claiming a numerical improvement without matched logs.

## 17. 其他常见综合题

### Why do you want to study in the Greater Bay Area?

> I have studied and worked in Shenzhen, and my robotics experience is connected to the region’s technology and manufacturing environment. Studying in the Greater Bay Area would let me continue building academic foundations while staying close to the kinds of robot systems I want to work on. I would choose a programme for its curriculum and learning opportunities, not only for its location.

### You applied to both Robotics, Computer Science and AI. Are you unsure about your direction?

> My long-term application area is robotics, but different programmes strengthen different parts of my preparation. Robotics programmes address mechanisms, estimation, planning and control. Computer Science programmes address software, data and distributed systems. AI programmes address learning and model evaluation. I am clear about the application area; I am comparing which foundation is the most useful next step for each programme.

### Which project are you most proud of?

> I would choose **[P1 / P5 / P6 / P4]**, depending on the programme. I am proud of it because **[specific responsibility]** connected to **[system-level outcome]**. The most important decision I made was **[decision]**. I can explain what was done and what evidence supports it, while being clear about the part that was team work.

选项目建议：机器人控制选 P1 或 P7；导航选 P5；AI 选 P4；CS/系统选 P6。不要把全国总决赛名次作为自己个人的项目成果。

### What if you receive an offer from another programme?

> I would compare the programmes against my learning goals: the technical foundations, the project or research options, the fit with my experience, and the kind of role I want after graduation. I would make a considered decision and communicate with each university according to its deadlines. I would not treat the decision as a ranking contest alone.

### What robotics topic are you currently interested in?

> I am interested in embodied robotics, especially how perception and state uncertainty affect physical action. In my projects, I saw that a planner or controller can only be as reliable as its inputs and execution limits. I want to study when learning-based components add value to model-based estimation and control, and how to evaluate them safely on real systems.

### How do you know you are ready for graduate-level study?

> My undergraduate coursework and projects gave me a foundation in computer science, control and robotics implementation. I also know the areas where I need more depth, including optimization, probability and machine-learning theory. I am prepared to review prerequisites, ask precise questions and work consistently rather than assuming that practical experience alone is enough.

### How would your professors or teammates describe you?

> I hope they would describe me as practical, responsible and willing to trace a problem across system boundaries. I try to communicate what I know and what I have not verified. To make this answer stronger, I would support it with a real example from a professor or teammate rather than listing adjectives alone.

## 11. 网上面试信息怎么用：练题，不押题

经验帖往往来自其他项目、年份或学位层级。把它当作“面试官可能顺着个人材料追问”的提醒，不能背成某校固定流程。官方当前说明、未公开事项和经验帖可信度统一看[网络面试信息核验与练习优先级](06_网络面试信息核验与练习优先级.md)。

### Q: What did you personally contribute to the project?

> I was responsible for [真实模块]. I implemented or analysed [具体动作], and coordinated with [真实合作对象 or interface]. The result I can support with my records is [真实证据]. I would distinguish this from the overall team outcome, which was [团队成果].

方括号必须按你的真实材料填写，不要照读模板。

### Q: What would you do differently if you repeated the project?

> I would define the test conditions and success metrics earlier. Then I would compare a simple baseline with the proposed method, log inputs and outputs with timestamps, and change one factor at a time. This would help me tell whether an improvement came from the algorithm or from a different test condition.

### Q: What if you do not know the answer to a technical question?

> I am not certain of the exact result. My current understanding is [我能确定的部分]. I would first state the assumptions, then check [model, units, boundary conditions or data] and verify it with a small calculation or a controlled test.
