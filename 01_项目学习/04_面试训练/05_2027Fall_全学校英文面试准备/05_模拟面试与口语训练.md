# 全英文模拟面试、反问与口语训练计划

> 面试问题是练习题，不代表任一学校一定会按此提问。训练目标是听懂、用自己的话回答、能承受连续追问。

## 1. 30 分钟模拟面试流程

|时间|面试内容|你的目标|
|---|---|---|
|0–2 分钟|问候、自我介绍|60–90 秒，不念完整简历|
|2–5 分钟|为什么申请该校/专业|讲 2 个官网核验点 + 1 个个人经历|
|5–12 分钟|一个主项目|输入→处理→输出→执行→验证|
|12–18 分钟|技术追问/白板题|单位、假设、限制、符号说清楚|
|18–22 分钟|团队/失败/压力题|STAR，个人贡献边界清晰|
|22–26 分钟|职业规划/学习缺口|短期现实、长期方向、补强办法|
|26–29 分钟|反问学校|问项目/课程/项目实践，不问已公开常识|
|29–30 分钟|结束|简洁致谢|

## 2. 完整问答模拟 A：机器人/自主系统

### Interviewer: Please introduce yourself.

使用[机器人方向 60 秒版](02_通用英文回答与自我介绍.md#3-60-秒自我介绍机器人机器人与自主系统版)。

### Interviewer: Which project best represents your preparation for this programme?

> I would choose the mobile-robot navigation project because it connected state estimation, planning and control on one vehicle. The system used IMU, wheel and LiDAR information for localization, Hybrid-A* for a kinematically feasible global path, TEB for local trajectory optimization, and MPC for tracking. My work covered the algorithm chain and its integration on the vehicle. One lesson was that timing and coordinate consistency matter as much as the planner itself. I would report performance numbers only if I had the matching evaluation log.

### Interviewer: Explain how Hybrid-A* differs from ordinary A*.

> Ordinary grid A* searches discrete cells and usually treats neighboring-cell moves as the action. Hybrid-A* also accounts for heading and vehicle motion primitives, so it can search for paths that are more consistent with a car’s turning constraints. It still discretizes states for search and needs collision checking and cost design. A path from Hybrid-A* is not automatically a time-parameterized trajectory.

### Interviewer: Why does MPC predict several steps ahead?

> MPC uses a model to predict how candidate control inputs may affect future states over a finite horizon. It chooses an input sequence that balances tracking error and control effort while respecting constraints. It executes only the first input, receives a new state estimate and solves again. This feedback makes it receding-horizon control rather than an open-loop plan.

### Interviewer: What would you do if the localization estimate jumps?

> I would first check the timestamp and frame of each input, motion direction and encoder scaling, and whether the map observation is consistent. Then I would separate sensor-data problems from estimator-model problems. I would log the innovation and state output and reproduce the case before tuning the filter. A single smooth-looking trajectory is not enough to prove localization accuracy.

## 3. 完整问答模拟 B：人工智能

### Interviewer: Tell me about your AI experience.

> I built a smart-gardening system using YOLOv5 inference on an RK3588 edge device, a LangGraph decision chain and a local irrigation state machine on a Hi3861 board. My work focused on connecting perception, decision and device execution. The project taught me that a confidence score is not a complete safety guarantee, so the system needs explicit rules and local actuator logic. I want to strengthen my foundations in machine learning and model evaluation.

### Interviewer: Did you train the model?

> My documented contribution focused on the inference and system-integration chain. I would only claim model training if I can identify the dataset, training configuration and changes I personally made. My main learning from the project was how to deploy and constrain a model inside an application.

### Interviewer: How would you improve the system?

> I would first collect representative data and define the task metric. Then I would measure model errors, confidence calibration, inference latency and failures under difficult lighting or occlusion. I would test the local state machine and manual override separately, and then evaluate the full camera-to-pump loop. I would not change several components at once because that would make the result hard to interpret.

## 4. 完整问答模拟 C：计算机科学/平台软件

### Interviewer: What is the main problem you addressed at Kaihong?

> I worked on making robot-platform functions more consistent across heterogeneous devices. My tasks included capability abstraction, command mapping, state handling and task coordination. The key issue was that a value needs its timestamp, frame and validity context, and a command needs to match what a device can actually do. The platform uses a policy-check and feedback chain so a proposed task is not treated as an authorized action by default.

### Interviewer: How do you design a good interface?

> I start by defining the meaning and unit of each field, valid ranges, timing and error states. I keep hardware-specific conversion at an adapter boundary and make failures explicit to the caller. Then I test nominal and invalid cases, including delayed or missing data. A shared message type is useful, but it does not make two devices semantically identical.

## 5. 综合高频追问清单

先口头回答，再看答案；每题不超过 90 秒。

### 学业与动机

1. Why did you choose your undergraduate major?
2. Which course has been most useful to your projects?
3. Which topic is currently the most difficult for you?
4. Why do you need a postgraduate degree now?
5. Why this school and not another one?
6. What will you contribute to the cohort?

### 项目与技术

7. What was the most difficult engineering issue you faced?
8. What did you personally implement?
9. What is one design decision you made and why?
10. What would happen if the sensor data became stale?
11. How did you test the system?
12. What is one limitation of your approach?
13. Which units and coordinate frames were involved?
14. What is the difference between your algorithm output and the motor command?
15. What would you change if you repeated the project?

### 研究与未来

16. What topic would you like to study in a capstone or thesis?
17. What baseline would you compare against?
18. What metrics would you use?
19. What would falsify your hypothesis?
20. What is your short-term career plan?
21. How would you handle not getting your first-choice role?

### 人际与责任

22. Describe a team experience.
23. Tell me about a disagreement.
24. How do you react to negative feedback?
25. Tell me about a failure.
26. How do you prioritize work under time pressure?
27. How do you make sure a robot action is safe?
28. What would you do if you discovered that a result was wrong?

## 6. 推荐给学校的反问

一次选 2–3 个，不要把所有问题连问：

- Which courses or project options are most relevant to students who want to work on robotics systems?
- How are capstone or thesis topics matched with supervisors?
- What practical resources or robot platforms are normally available to students?
- How does the programme help students connect coursework with experimental or industry projects?
- What mathematical or software foundations would you recommend that incoming students review?
- For my cohort, which programme structure or course list should applicants use as the current version?

避免问：官网首页已经写明的学制/地点/课程名；“我一定能录取吗”；把面试官当招生办问尚未公开的个人申请结果。

## 7. 听不清、没听懂、需要思考时的英语句子

### 请求重复/澄清

> Could you please repeat the last part of the question?

> Could I confirm whether you are asking about the algorithm design or the hardware implementation?

### 给自己几秒钟

> Let me think about that for a moment.

> I will answer this in two parts: the model-level idea and the real-system limitation.

### 不知道但仍然回答得负责

> I am not certain about that detail. My current understanding is ____. I would verify it in the relevant source or documentation before making a definite claim.

### 纠正自己的表达

> Let me correct one point. The controller produced an intermediate command; the driver converted it into the hardware-specific command.

## 8. 两周口语准备计划

### 第 1–3 天：事实与主线

- 选定每所学校对应的 CV/PS 版本和确切专业名。
- 用中文讲清 90 秒个人主线、三个实习和两个最强项目。
- 把个人贡献、团队工作、教学推导与实测分开列出来。

### 第 4–6 天：学校动机

- 每天练 2 所学校的 Why school/Why programme，每题 60 秒。
- 每个答案必须包含 2 个官网事实、1 个真实经历、1 个学习缺口。
- 练“为什么不选另一个申请方向”的区分答案。

### 第 7–10 天：技术追问

- 机器人方向：P1、P2、P5、P7。
- CS 方向：P6、P4、P3 实时软件边界。
- AI 方向：P4、P6 加模型评估、数据、数学缺口。
- 练“输入—处理—输出—执行—验证”五段，不从公式开始背。

### 第 11–12 天：行为题

- 录制团队、失败、压力、反馈、安全四类回答。
- 对真实冲突/失败故事做事实核对；没有故事就不编。
- 练一个回答如何自然承认限制。

### 第 13 天：完整模拟

- 用 30 分钟完整流程录音，另做一个 15 分钟精简版。
- 记录：答非所问、超时、专业名错误、单位混淆、过度承诺。

### 第 14 天：轻量复习

- 只看每所学校一页速记、个人事实卡和易错边界。
- 不临时学习大量新术语；保证能用短句解释最熟的项目。

如果面试时间不足 14 天，按顺序优先：事实/学校名 → 自我介绍 → 两个主项目 → 技术追问 → 行为题 → 反问。

## 9. 自评表：每题 0–2 分

|项目|0 分|1 分|2 分|
|---|---|---|---|
|切题|没有回答问题|部分回答|第一句就给结论|
|事实准确|夸大或混淆|基本准确但边界少|贡献、版本和限制清楚|
|技术逻辑|只报术语|有步骤但跳步|输入、处理、输出、验证清楚|
|英语表达|句子过长/听不懂|可理解但犹豫|短句有层次，可接追问|
|时间管理|超时|接近上限|核心 45–90 秒，追问再展开|

复盘时只挑一个技术错误和一个英语表达问题先改，不要把答案背成文章。

## 7. 按实际招生信息练三轮模拟

### 第一轮：通用筛选（约15分钟）

60秒自我介绍、Why this school/programme、一个代表项目、未来规划、反问学校。检查结论是否先说、每题是否控制在30–90秒、内容是否与提交CV/PS一致。

### 第二轮：技术追问（20–30分钟）

沿一条工程链连续追问输入/单位/坐标系、模型/算法、输出/下游、失败/边界、验证/指标。例如 P2 从手柄 SE(3) 追到坐标标定、末端误差、Jacobian、IK 和关节命令；P5 从定位状态追到全局路、局部轨迹、MPC控制量和车辆反馈。

不会公式时先说变量和假设，再用小例子检查单位与方向，不乱猜。

### 第三轮：学校定制（10–20分钟）

只依据申请文件和学校公开事实追问。重点复核 PolyU 入学批次、CUHK-SZ 项目正式名称、CityU P53 英语面试条件、MUST 口试、HKUST(GZ) 学位类型。实际邀请邮件决定形式和语言。

### 自评分（每项0–2分）

|项目|0|1|2|
|---|---|---|---|
|事实准确|与材料矛盾/编造|大致正确但模糊|项目名、批次、个人贡献均可核对|
|结构|绕、无结论|有结论但跳跃|结论→证据→局限完整|
|技术|只报名词|因果解释不全|输入、计算、输出、单位/接口连通|
|英语|难以听懂|基本可懂但冗长|短句、关键词明确|
|诚信边界|团队/仿真说成个人实测|边界含糊|个人、团队、仿真、实测明确区分|

每轮只优先修订三项，不要因为搜到更多面经就无限扩题。学校官方信息和面经可信度见[面试信息核验页](06_网络面试信息核验与练习优先级.md)。
