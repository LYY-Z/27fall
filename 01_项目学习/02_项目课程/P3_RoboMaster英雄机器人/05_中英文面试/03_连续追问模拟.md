# P3 连续追问模拟

训练方式：先不看答案口述，再看下面参考。每轮约10分钟，记录哪一步从理解变成背词。

**初学者练法：**第一遍只用中文讲清楚因果，不要求马上说出所有术语；卡住时停下来画对象、箭头和信号单位。第二遍再回答英文追问。先说具体机构做什么，再讲它看什么反馈、最后发什么电机命令。配置的任务周期和测得周期、控制器定义和活动调用必须分开。先用[项目白话总览](../00_项目全貌与学习路线/03_P3端到端任务与反馈链.md)把主线复述一遍。

## Round 1

**Interviewer:** What control problems does the hero robot have?

**Candidate:** The robot combines continuous tracking with discrete mechanisms. The gimbal tracks orientation, the friction wheels maintain speed, the feeder completes a shot cycle, and the chassis moves within available power.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Which subsystem did you own personally?

**回答依据：** 云台要跟踪方向，摩擦轮要稳定速度，拨弹要完成离散动作，底盘要在功率受限时移动；这些模块通过模式和通信协调。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 2

**Interviewer:** Why does the gimbal use cascaded loops?

**Candidate:** The outer angle loop generates a rate reference. The inner rate loop uses gyro feedback to control fast motion. I would stabilize the inner loop first and then tune the outer loop.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What happens if the outer loop is too aggressive?

**回答依据：** 角度环输出角速度参考；角速度环用陀螺反馈抑制快速运动和扰动。内环先稳定，外环再调，频率快不等于闭环带宽大。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 3

**Interviewer:** How do you handle an angle crossing the boundary?

**Candidate:** I wrap the angle error to the shortest signed difference. For a target of 179 degrees and a measurement of minus 179 degrees, the error is minus 2 degrees.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Would this work for a joint with a mechanical stop?

**回答依据：** 用最短角差，目标179当前-179是-2度；源码yaw环按-180到180环绕，其他弧度接口不能混用。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 4

**Interviewer:** Which launcher controller is active in the snapshot?

**Candidate:** The active friction-wheel path calls three sliding-mode controllers with an outer sign reversal. The variable-gain PID calculations are commented out in this snapshot.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Could a different deployed version use PID?

**回答依据：** launcher.c三路-smc_controller实际调用；VSP参数和初始化存在但计算调用注释，不能因文件有PID就说当前启用。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 5

**Interviewer:** Why use a boundary layer in sliding-mode control?

**Candidate:** A saturation boundary layer softens switching near zero and can reduce chattering. It trades exact switching behavior for a smoother command and possible residual error.

**Interviewer, deeper:** How would you choose the boundary-layer width?

**回答依据：** 硬符号切换对噪声敏感产生抖振；sat(s/epsilon)在零附近连续，但允许残差。源码内部u符号与外部负号须一起看。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 6

**Interviewer:** Why does the power limiter solve a quadratic equation?

**Candidate:** Scaling the command can produce quadratic loss terms and linear mechanical-power terms. This leads to a quadratic expression in the scale factor. Its accuracy depends on the coefficient model.

**Interviewer, deeper:** How would you validate the model coefficients?

**回答依据：** 电流或力矩随缩放k改变，损耗近似含k平方项，机械项含k项，于是得到ak平方+bk+c。系数模型要标定。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 7

**Interviewer:** Does the available limiter guarantee a hard power bound?

**Candidate:** Not automatically. After solving for a scale factor, the source applies a restoration toward one. I would check the final command and measured power rather than infer a hard guarantee from the initial quadratic solve.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What would you change to enforce a strict bound?

**回答依据：** 源码解k后又按kparam向1恢复，最终输出可能越过原约束，模型和裁判延迟也影响；须检查最终预测和实测。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 8

**Interviewer:** What should happen during communication loss or relax mode?

**Candidate:** The system needs explicit safe outputs and controller-state handling. In the launcher relax path, four current commands are cleared. Other modules must be inspected separately rather than assumed to behave identically.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What must be checked before re-enabling?

**回答依据：** 停止危险动作并明确输出零或禁用，清积分和模式状态；源码Relax把发射四路电流归零，其他模块要分别追踪。 追问需要回模型、源调用或实验条件；不要自动添加个人性能数字。

## Round 9：SMC 与变比例 PID 的证据边界

**Interviewer:** Is the active friction-wheel controller a standard, proven sliding-mode design?

**Candidate:** The inspected source forms a sliding variable from RPM error and its finite difference, uses a saturation boundary layer, limits the output, and the caller reverses the sign. That shows the implemented calculation. It does not by itself prove the standard reaching condition or robustness; I would need the motor model, verified sign, consistent units, sampling behavior and disturbance bounds.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Why mention units if the code compiles?

**回答依据：** 编译器只检查类型和语法，不检查物理量纲。源码里误差是RPM、差分是RPM/s，而 `give_current` 是协议整数。读者要核对中间缩放，不因数字能相加就认为物理单位一致。回[SMC与VSP详解](../01_原理与子系统/03_摩擦轮拨弹PID与滑模版本.md)。

## Round 10：从 VSP 参数谈到实际可运行路径

**Interviewer:** How would you tune the variable-gain PID parameters?

**Candidate:** I would first choose the desired proportional gain at representative small and large speed errors, fit the logarithmic curve, and then validate the output, noise and saturation under the same limits. In this snapshot, the VSP calculations are commented out, so I would treat that as an isolated experiment rather than the active launcher path.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What could go wrong near zero error?

**回答依据：** 这条源码 include 路径中的 `user_lib.h` 把 `abs(x)` 定义为浮点可用宏，所以不能说浮点误差会先被截成整数。误差为零时 `log10f(0)` 会得到负无穷，后续最小增益比较会处理它；还需核对对数输入有效性、最大增益上限、输出限幅及离散 I/D 与控制周期的关系。

## Round 11：控制器周期与真实频率

**Interviewer:** The RTOS tick is configured at 1 kHz. Does that mean the gimbal loop runs at 1 kHz?

**Candidate:** No. That is the tick configuration. The actual loop period also includes execution, relative or absolute delay behavior, pre-emption, blocking and interrupts. I would timestamp the same point in the loop and report the measured mean, range, jitter and missed deadlines.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What is the difference between `vTaskDelay` and `vTaskDelayUntil`?

**回答依据：** 前者相对当前调用时刻等 tick，后者基于上次计划唤醒时刻排固定节拍；后者能减少工作耗时累积漂移，但都不能消除任务超期或调度迟到。回[FreeRTOS周期详解](../01_原理与子系统/01_硬件反馈与FreeRTOS任务.md)。

## Round 12：从 IMU 原始字节讲到姿态估计

**Interviewer:** Does the IMU transmit yaw, pitch, roll or a quaternion?

**Candidate:** The sensor provides accelerometer and gyroscope measurements through registers. SPI+DMA transfers those bytes; the driver converts and remaps them. The AHRS integrates gyro data and corrects tilt from the gravity direction to estimate a quaternion, then the software extracts Euler angles for the controller. Without a valid heading reference, yaw can drift.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What does initializing the quaternion to `[1, 0, 0, 0]` establish?

**回答依据：** 它把解算器当前姿态设为起始参考，不表示传感器已经测得绝对水平或地理北。之后每个更新周期继续用陀螺和加速度观测修正；源码未使用有效磁力计时，yaw没有磁北约束。回[IMU到AHRS完整链](../01_原理与子系统/02_云台角度速度环与视觉仲裁.md)。

## Round 13：堵转检测、轮速分配与功率限制

**Interviewer:** How does the source detect a feeder jam, and does it stop the motor immediately?

**Candidate:** It compares the absolute difference between the feeder's target and measured accumulated encoder positions with a 5,000-count threshold and an approximately one-second action window. A timed-out single-shot move enters `SHOOT_BLOCK`; the next control pass shifts the target by the configured reverse offset and enters `SHOOT_BLOCK_BACK`. The inspected code does not explicitly clear feeder current in the BLOCK branch, so I would not claim it stops before reversing.

**Interviewer, deeper:** What happens after the reverse attempt also times out?

**Candidate:** The state becomes `SHOOT_FAIL`. I can confirm one reverse-position attempt in this source. I would trace the final CAN and driver timeout paths before claiming that FAIL guarantees a safe stop.

**Interviewer:** What is the order from chassis velocity request to motor command?

**Candidate:** The source smooths the chassis velocity references, maps them to four motor RPM references in RF/LF/RB/LB order, applies a common wheel-speed scale if a target exceeds the configured RPM ceiling, applies the power-related scale, runs one speed PID per wheel, and sends the current-command fields over CAN. The CAN packing order must be checked against the wheel array because the rear-wheel arguments are reordered.

**Interviewer, deeper:** Why is the power prediction quadratic, and does the root guarantee the measured power stays below the referee limit?

**Candidate:** The estimated current is linear in a common scale factor, while the source includes a current-squared term, so expansion produces a quadratic. The root is only as reliable as its model and branch handling. The function also relaxes the solved scale toward one, and the low-buffer trigger assignment is commented out, so I would validate final RPM references and synchronized electrical measurements instead of claiming a hard guarantee.

**回答依据：** 回看[拨盘编码器状态机](../01_原理与子系统/03_摩擦轮拨弹PID与滑模版本.md)、[源码麦轮映射](../01_原理与子系统/04_麦轮运动学与轮速反馈.md)、[功率预测推导](../01_原理与子系统/05_功率缓冲与输出缩放.md)和[一个周期的控制顺序](../02_源码阅读与运行/02_一个周期的调用与数据流.md)。回答时要把源码事实、设计建议和实机验证分开。

## 复盘答案标准

每问记录：公式/单位是否正确，能否指出函数，个人动作是否明确，验证是否有数据，限制是否诚实，英文是否可理解。不会则明确不知道的部分并解释已知部分。下次优先补一项，不改写成“已掌握”。

## Round 14：最容易漏掉的实现边界

**Interviewer:** Does Fire_ON prove the friction wheels are ready to fire?

**Candidate:** It sets their RPM references. I did not find a three-wheel speed-readiness gate in the inspected firing condition. Such a gate would need fresh feedback, defined speed tolerances and a holding time.

**Interviewer:** Are the three VSP coefficients independent?

**Candidate:** In the logarithmic region, k1 and k2 combine into one offset. I fix the error scale, fit the slope and baseline, and check gain and command bounds.

**Interviewer:** What happens when an absolute wake time is already in the past?

**Candidate:** The delay call returns without blocking. The loop may run successive late iterations until its planned wake time catches up. That does not recreate missed sensor samples or prove the deadlines were met.

**Interviewer:** Is the pitch-loop angle simply the motor-relative angle?

**Candidate:** The active angle loop reads the sum of an IMU-derived field and a chassis-board angle. The encoder-relative angle is a separate field. I verify axis and reference conventions before assigning a physical meaning to that sum.
